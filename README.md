# JOb Market(2025) Analysis
## Project Overview

This project analyzes a 2025 job market dataset across different categories. It compares average salaries by job role and contract type, and identifies country-wise and monthly hiring patterns.
Special focus is given to USA job market to understand which roles are in high demand, which jobs offer higher salaries and how full-time and part-time salaries differ.



## Dataset Source 
The dataset used in this project was sourced from Kaggle. It contains job posting data for 2025 from multiple countries with details on job category, salary, contract type, and posting date.
## Objectives of the Project
- Analyze job demand across diffrent job categories
- Compare average salaries by job category
- Compare full time VS part-time sararies
- Identify top-paying job categories in the USA
- Analyze monthly job posting trends
## Data Analysis Steps
- Cleaned the dataset handeling missing and unknown values, dropping unnecessery columns, checking duplicates values and renaming columns.
- Filtered job posting specific to the USA
- Calculated average salaries by job category
- Identified top 10 jobs by average salary
- Analyzed monthly job posting trends
- Compared average salary by contract time
- Visualized finding using charts
## Key Findings
- Healthcare & Nursing jobs are in high demand in 2025.
- In USA Healthcare & Nursing, Customer service, Travel and IT jobs are most in demand.
- The highest paying jobs in the USA are Legal, Accounting & Finance, and IT, followed by Teaching and Advertising.
- Most job posting in USA are after middle of the year with peak in September.
- Full-time jobs pay more than Part-time jobs, except in Social work and IT jobs.
## Tools & Libraries used
- **Python** : Programming language used for data processing and analysis
- **Pandas** : Library used for data cleaning, manipulation and analysis
- **Matplotlib** : For visulization and showing insights through chart
